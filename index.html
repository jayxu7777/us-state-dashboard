Caveats · ZORI is the largest metro per state (NY=NYC, CA=LA, TX=Dallas). State CES wages are NSA (BLS does not publish state-level SA AHE). EIA monthly state retail gasoline only covers CA, TX, FL, NY, MA, MN, OH, WA, CO — other states are blank. RPP and GDP are annual/quarterly — the latest may lag 6–18 months. FHFA HPI is a cumulative repeat-sales index (1980Q1=100) so the level reflects 45 years of appreciation, not absolute price. Philly Fed coincident index is monthly but not published for DC.
